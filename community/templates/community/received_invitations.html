How Invitations Work

Private development groups are invitation-only. Group owners invite registered developers, and each developer decides whether to join.

Accepting an invitation adds you to the group. Declining an invitation does not affect your existing group memberships.

Private development groups support a maximum of 15 members, including the owner.
